## How to check properly (about an hour)
1. Search the name with "sabzi / vegetables / grocery / delivery" on Google, Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp Business, Google Maps (Udaipur and India), the Play Store and App Store.
2. Search the Indian trademark register (IP India public search) in classes 29, 31, 35, 39; ask a trademark attorney before relying on your own search.
3. Check `.in` / `.com` domains and Instagram/Facebook/YouTube handles.
4. Say it aloud to five people in Udaipur and ask them to write it down: count spelling variants ("Haat" / "Hat" / "Hatt").
