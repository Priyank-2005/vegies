# Sales & Marketing Plan

> Status: DRAFT v0.1 · 2026-10-23. Budgets, conversion rates and CAC are **provisional [A]** to be replaced by pilot data. No brand name, logo or domain is final (placeholder "FreshVan"); do not register anything before availability/trademark checks (DECISIONS D-012).
> Guardrails: no spam, no purchased contact lists, no fake reviews, no health/organic claims we can't prove, no auto-recurring charges without explicit authorization.

## 1. Strategy in one paragraph

Win **one dense neighbourhood at a time** through trusted local channels (housing societies, WhatsApp communities, referrals), prove **paid, repeated orders at a published stop**, and only then spend on paid ads. Because route economics need ~12+ orders per stop (UNIT_ECONOMICS), marketing's job is to **concentrate demand at specific stops and slots**, not to maximise website traffic.

## 2. Funnel and metrics

```
Awareness → Serviceability check → Product view → Add to cart → Checkout start → Paid order → Collected order → 2nd order → Referral
```

| Stage | Metric | Source | Pilot target [A] |
|---|---|---|---|
| Reach | Society/community members reached | Manual + UTM | tracked |
| Interest | Serviceability checks / landing sign-ups | App events | 300 verified in 3 societies |
| Consideration | Product views, add-to-cart rate | Analytics | measure |
| Conversion | Checkout conversion; **paid & fulfilled orders** | DB | measure; ≥ 25 paid pre-orders at one stop |
| Retention | First→second order; 4-week repeat | DB | ≥ 40–45% |
| Referral | Referral conversion; K-factor | DB | ≥ 0.2 |
| Economics | CAC; contribution margin by channel | DB + spend log | CAC ≤ ₹150 |
| Quality | Complaints & refunds per 100 orders | Support log | ≤ 8 contacts, refunds ≤ 3% |

**Rule:** a channel is judged on *paid fulfilled orders* and *repeat rate*, never on likes, views or survey enthusiasm.

## 3. Neighbourhood-first launch & selection

Use the scoring rubric in BUSINESS_PLAN §7. Ground-truth steps (all free):
1. Map candidate societies (≥ 200 homes within walking distance of a possible stop). 2. Speak to 3–5 residents and 2 RWA office-bearers per candidate. 3. Check van access/parking in person at the intended time. 4. Count existing vendor/store/quick-commerce coverage by talking to guards and residents. 5. Pick the 3 best candidates; launch one.

Expected orders per stop = households in reach × sign-up rate × order rate. Fill with *measured* ratios from the landing-page test; I deliberately give no default ratios here.

### A. Housing societies
- **Plan:** RWA/secretary pitch (see §11 asset), 10-minute demo basket tasting, request a fixed 30-min stop at the main gate, get a one-page written permission (day/time/parking spot/rules). Offer the society a "community credit" (e.g., ₹10 per first-time order credited to a society fund) if legally and accounting-wise acceptable **[?]**.
- **Metric:** sign-ups per 100 homes; orders per stop.
- **Budget:** ₹3–5k (sample basket, printouts).
- **Decision rule:** if < 8 paid orders by week 3 at a stop, change stop/timing or drop society.

### B. WhatsApp community groups
- Post only with admin permission; no unsolicited mass-adding. Share the schedule, a weekly rate card photo, and order cut-off reminder.
- **Metric:** orders attributable by code/UTM; opt-outs.

### C. WhatsApp Business catalogue & broadcasts
- Utility messages (confirmations, reminders) permitted transactionally; **promotional messages only to opted-in customers** with easy opt-out. Reported India marketing-message rate ~₹0.86–1.09 vs utility ~₹0.115–0.145 **[V, secondary; verify on Meta's rate card]** → ~1 broadcast/week/customer is affordable (< ₹5/month/customer).
- **Metric:** order conversion per broadcast; unsubscribe rate (< 2% per send).

### D. Referral programme
- Reward: "give ₹X, get ₹X" **after the referred friend's first order is collected and not refunded** (anti-abuse). Cap per referrer per month; same address/phone/device flagged.
- **Cost check:** if contribution per order is ₹40–100, a ₹50+₹50 reward is only affordable if the friend orders ≥ 3 times; start at ₹30+₹30 or "free fruit bag worth ₹40 cost" and measure.
- **Metric:** referral conversion; cost per referred repeat customer.

### E–K (summary)
- **Flyers/QR:** only where permitted (society notice boards, shops that agree). QR links to the society landing page with UTM per location.
- **Google Business Profile:** create after legal entity + address clarity; list service stops as service areas; collect *genuine* reviews only (E-Commerce Rules prohibit fake reviews).
- **Instagram/Reels:** show actual sourcing, weighing, packing; "today's rate board"; avoid unverifiable claims ("pesticide-free", "organic") unless certified **[?]**.
- **Micro-influencers:** 2–3 trial partnerships only after the first stop works; pay in baskets + referral codes; disclose partnership.
- **Meta ads:** start ₹300–500/day radius-targeted, 2 creatives, optimise on *paid order* events once ≥ 50 conversions; kill if CAC > ₹200 for 2 weeks.
- **Offices/hostels/PG:** pre-order weekly baskets; payment up-front.

## 6. Pricing and offers (test plan; margin first)

| Test | Hypothesis | Margin guard | Decision rule |
|---|---|---|---|
| Everyday price parity with local supermarket | Price-aware shoppers will switch | Floor price ≥ cost + 12% | Keep if repeat ≥ 45% |
| Price-per-kg comparison badges | Transparency builds trust | Only against verified prices we recorded | Show only with dated source |
| Family veg basket (e.g., 5–6 kg staple mix) | Raises AOV, simplifies choice | Bundle margin ≥ 26% | Keep if AOV ≥ ₹450 |
| Fruit basket / weekly essentials | Raises frequency | Margin ≥ 25% | |
| Seasonal offers | Move surplus, attract | Cap discount 10% on one SKU/day | |
| First-order incentive | Lower activation barrier | ≤ ₹50 or 10% (whichever lower), one per phone/address | Keep if 2nd-order rate ≥ 40% |
| Referral credits | See D | See D | |
| Recurring weekly basket (customer-controlled) | Smooths demand | No discount initially | Skip/edit/cancel one tap |
| Free collection vs paid delivery | Free collection pulls density | Delivery fee ≥ true cost share | Delivery only inside served societies |

Margin impact: each 1% of discounts costs ≈ ₹4 per ₹400 order; the 10% discount scenario alone moves a route-shift by ≈ −₹1,150 (UNIT_ECONOMICS §7.2). **No blanket discounts.** Offer targeted, capped, time-boxed incentives and track *second-order margin*.

## 7. Retention

- **Reorder in two taps:** "Last order" and "Favourites"; cart prefilled; slot suggestion.
- **Saved baskets / weekly basket** (customer-created; reminder by WhatsApp **with consent**; one-tap skip; *no automatic charge*; payment required each time or explicit authorized mandate later after legal/payment review).
- **Reminders:** cutoff-aware ("Order by 9 PM for tomorrow's 6 PM van").
- **Recommendations:** based on own purchase history only (simple "you usually buy" list).
- **Loyalty:** defer points systems; start with "5th order free fruit bag" only if margins allow.
- **Feedback:** 1-tap rating + comment after pickup; flag low ratings to support within hours.
- **Transparency:** freshness promise, weight transparency, clear refund policy page.

## 8. Neighbourhood ambassador programme

Trusted residents help introduce the service.
- **Role:** share schedule, collect sign-ups, help at stop during peak. Not an employee; no cash handling.
- **Reward model [A]:** ₹30 credit per verified new household that completes a collected order + a monthly basket credit capped at ₹300 if ≥ 10 verified households. Cost per new household ≈ ₹30–40 → within CAC target.
- **Abuse controls:** referred accounts must have distinct phone, delivery stop and device patterns; rewards released after the referred order's refund window; manual review above thresholds.
- **Legal:** treat as referral rewards, not income-bearing employment; get CA advice if amounts become material **[?]**.

## 9. WhatsApp & CRM

- Use a Meta-approved BSP; record opt-in (timestamp, text, source), honour opt-out immediately.
- Keep the **website as primary ordering channel in MVP**; WhatsApp is for notifications, reminders, support, reorder links.
- CRM = DB tables: customer, consent, order history, tags (stop, preferences), campaign log. No separate CRM tool initially.
- Messaging categories: utility (always on), service/support (24 h window replies), promotional (opt-in only).

## 10. Measurement and weekly growth review

**Weekly meeting (45 min, same slot):**
1. Scorecard: visitors → serviceability → add-to-cart → paid → collected → repeat (by stop/channel).
2. CAC and contribution margin by channel.
3. Complaints/refunds and root causes.
4. Experiments: each has **hypothesis, budget, target metric, decision rule, owner, end date** (template below).
5. Decisions: scale / iterate / kill; update the experiment log.

**Experiment card template**
```
Name:
Hypothesis: If we <change>, then <metric> will <move> because <reason>.
Budget (₹) / time box:
Primary metric & target:
Guardrail metrics (margin, refunds, opt-outs):
Decision rule: scale if..., kill if...
Result / learnings:
```

Attribution: UTM + `ref` codes per society/ambassador/channel stored on the order; QR codes include location code. Sampling bias acknowledged (many orders come from word-of-mouth without codes; ask "How did you hear about us?" at checkout, optional).

## 11. Sales assets (drafts; to be finalised after brand/domain checks)

**Positioning statement:** *"Pre-order your week's fruits and vegetables. We buy fresh that morning, weigh honestly, and bring them to a stop near your home."*

**Tagline options (unvetted, check conflicts):**
1. "Ordered tonight. Fresh by evening."
2. "Fresh from the market, at your gate."
3. "Weighed honestly. Priced clearly."
4. "Your neighbourhood sabzi stop."

**Brand story (draft):** Local produce markets are full of freshness, but also full of uncertainty about weight, price and time. We started as neighbours who wanted a simple promise: tell us what you need by tonight, we'll buy it fresh tomorrow, weigh it honestly and meet you at your gate.

**Society partnership pitch (one page):**
- What: scheduled fresh produce stop, 30 minutes, 2 days/week.
- Benefits to residents: fresh, transparent pricing, no delivery fee, pre-order, no minimums.
- Society asks: gate access for a parked van, a notice-board post, a point of contact.
- Our commitments: on-time arrival, no noise/idling, staff ID, hygiene, refund guarantee, grievance contact, orderly queue.
- Optional: community credit, free demo basket day.

**WhatsApp introduction (opt-in only):**
> "Hi {name}, thanks for joining {brand}. Next fresh-produce stop at {society}: {day} {time}. Order by {cutoff}: {link}. Reply STOP anytime to opt out."

**Launch announcement (society group, with admin approval):**
> "Fresh vegetables and fruits at our gate every {day} {time}. See the rate card and pre-order by {cutoff}: {link}. Pay online or at the van. Weighed in front of you; refund if anything is not right."

**Referral message:**
> "Try {brand}: you and I both get ₹{x} off after your first collected order. {code} / {link}"

**Social content calendar (first 4 weeks, 3 posts/week):** Mon rate board photo/reel; Wed packing/weighing process; Fri weekend basket; Sunday customer story (with consent). Themes: sourcing morning, weight honesty, seasonal item spotlight, how to store, recipes with in-basket items.

**Customer feedback survey (5 questions):** freshness (1–5), weight satisfaction (1–5), price (1–5), stop timing convenience (1–5), "what should we add?" (text) + NPS.
