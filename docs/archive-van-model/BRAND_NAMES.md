# Brand name shortlist (working document)

> **None of these has been checked** for trademark conflicts, domain availability, social handles or existing businesses in India. Do not print, register or publish any of them until that check is done (DECISIONS D-024). "FreshVan" in the app is a placeholder only.

## Criteria
Easy to say and spell for Hindi and English speakers · short · works in Devanagari and Roman script · suggests fresh produce/market, not just "van" (we may add delivery later) · not tied to Udaipur so it can expand · friendly, not corporate.

## Shortlist

| # | Name | Devanagari | Meaning / feel | Pros | Watch-outs |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Tokri** | टोकरी | "basket" | Very short, warm, instantly understood, perfect for a pre-ordered basket; works as "Tokri Taaza" | Common word: many small businesses may use it; trademark risk |
| 2 | **Hari Haat** | हरी हाट | "green market" | Says exactly what it is (haat = local market); lovely rhythm | Two words; "Haat" brands exist |
| 3 | **Dhaniya** | धनिया | Coriander, the free garnish every Indian shopper asks for | Playful and memorable; lets you do a signature "free dhaniya with every order" | Not obviously "fresh produce" to a stranger; may suit a tagline better than a name |
| 4 | **Taaza Stop** | ताज़ा स्टॉप | "fresh stop" | Describes the model (a stop for fresh produce); bilingual | "Stop" feels transport-y; check "Taaza" brands |
| 5 | **Sabzi Saathi** | सब्ज़ी साथी | "vegetable companion" | Friendly; alliteration; Hindi-first | Long-ish; "Saathi" is widely used |
| 6 | **Mewar Mandi** | मेवाड़ मंडी | Mewar is Udaipur's region | Strong local pride and trust at launch | Limits expansion beyond Rajasthan; fine if you accept a regional brand |
| 7 | **Gate Pe Taaza** | गेट पे ताज़ा | "fresh at the gate" | Quirky, describes the collection-at-the-gate promise | Informal; hard to trademark as a phrase; maybe a tagline |
| 8 | **Aangan** | आँगन | "courtyard" | Evokes home and community; premium feel | Very common word; does not say produce |

## My ranking for your situation (a pre-order, collect-at-your-gate, trust-first business)
1. **Tokri** (best overall if available; add descriptor "Tokri: Taaza Sabzi & Fal")
2. **Hari Haat**
3. **Mewar Mandi** (best if you want strong local identity and are fine staying regional initially)

Tagline pairings: *Tokri: "Raat ko order, shaam ko taaza."* · *Hari Haat: "Tola hua, taaza, aapke gate par."* · *Mewar Mandi: "Apni mandi, apne mohalle mein."* (Check wording with a native Hindi/Mewari speaker.)

## How to check (cheap, takes about an hour per name)
1. Search the name + "sabzi / vegetables / grocery" on Google, Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp Business directory, Google Maps (Udaipur and India).
2. Search the Indian trademark register (IP India public search) for the word in classes 29, 31, 35, 39. Ask a trademark attorney before relying on a self-search.
3. Check `.in` and `.com` domains and the Instagram/Facebook/YouTube handles.
4. Say it aloud to 5 people in Udaipur, ask them to write it down: count spelling variants.
