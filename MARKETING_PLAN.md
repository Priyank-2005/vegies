# Sales & Marketing Plan: Hari Haat delivery (Udaipur)

> Status: v2 · 2026-10-24 · Rewritten for on-demand delivery. The society/van-stop plan is archived. Budgets, rates and CAC are **[A]**. No brand, logo or domain is final: check availability and trademarks first (DECISIONS D-024).
> Guardrails: no spam, no purchased lists, no fake reviews, no unprovable "organic / pesticide-free" claims, no auto-recurring charges without explicit authorisation.

## 1. Strategy
One city, one hub, one clear promise: **"Fresh sabzi and fruit at your door: pick Instant (₹20), 1 hour (₹10) or 3 hours (free)."** Win through trust (honest weights, easy refunds), the free-if-you-can-wait speed, and Hindi-first service: not through deep discounts, because contribution per order is thin (`UNIT_ECONOMICS.md`). Marketing's job is to raise **basket size** and **repeat rate**, not just orders.

## 2. Funnel and metrics
Visit → shared location → served ✓ → add to cart → checkout → order placed → delivered on time → reorder within 14 days → referral.
| Metric | Source | Early target [A] |
|---|---|---|
| Location-share rate (visitors who tap the button) | App events | measure |
| Served rate (inside our reach) | Serviceability checks | measure; low = wrong ads/areas |
| Cart → paid/placed conversion | DB | measure first |
| Median basket, share of orders by speed | DB | ≥ ₹300 → ₹380 |
| On-time within promise | DB | ≥ 90% |
| Repeat within 14 days | DB | ≥ 40% |
| CAC by channel; contribution per order by channel | Spend log + DB | CAC ≤ ₹150 |
| Waitlist sign-ups by area | Waitlist table | ≥ 150 per area to consider expansion |

A channel is judged on **delivered, repeated, profitable orders**, never clicks.

## 3. Pre-launch (4 weeks)
1. Landing page + the working shop in "closed" mode (the app already shows opening hours and a browse-only mode).
2. Sample orders from friends/family (the soft launch; cap Instant and 1-hour capacity low).
3. Price-basket benchmark and photo assets (real product photography; no misleading stock photos).
4. Society/WhatsApp-group introductions with admin consent (no mass-adding).
5. Measure: basket size, speed mix, delivery time, wastage, cash reconciliation. Decision memo after 2-3 weeks.

## 5. Pricing and offers (margin first)
- Delivery fees are fixed by the brief. **Do not stack free delivery + discounts**; the 3-hour option is already free.
- Offers that raise the basket: "Free coriander and chillies on orders above ₹X" (garnish culture; cheap to give), family bundles (when built), weekly essentials list, fruit baskets, seasonal highlights.
- First-order incentive: small and capped (≤ ₹30 or free garnish); one per phone/address; judged on second-order rate.
- A minimum order for the free speed is a pricing decision to test, not a marketing gimmick (D-034).
- Target price perception: near supermarket for staples; premium for freshness only where true.

## 6. Retention
Reorder in two taps (last basket), favourites, weekly basket created by the customer (reminder with consent; no auto-charge), cutoff-free ordering with speed choice, feedback after delivery, quick resolution of complaints, transparent refund and weight policy. Loyalty points deferred until margins are known.

## 7. WhatsApp and consent
Utility messages (confirmation, rider on the way, delivered, delay) are transactional; promotional messages only to people who opted in with recorded text/time/source. Use a Meta-approved provider; honour opt-out instantly. DPDP duties (substantive from 13 May 2027 [V, secondary]) → build consent logs now.

## 8. Measurement routine
Weekly 45-minute review: funnel by channel, basket and speed mix, on-time %, refunds/complaints, wastage, contribution per order. Every campaign has a hypothesis, budget, target metric, guardrail (margin, refunds) and a kill rule.
